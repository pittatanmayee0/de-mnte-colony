HISTORY OF De Monte colony

De Monte Colony, in the Alwarpet area of Chennai, traces its story back to the 19th century, when the area was associated with **John De Monte**, a Portuguese merchant who owned a large stretch of property there. According to accounts published about the colony, De Monte was believed to have lived a troubled personal life. Local stories say that his wife suffered from mental illness and that their only son died or disappeared, after which tragedy reportedly struck the family. These stories eventually became the foundation of the colony's haunted reputation. As the years passed, some of the properties were left vacant or poorly maintained. The dark lanes, overgrown vegetation, abandoned-looking houses and lack of lighting created an atmosphere that was perfect for frightening rumours. Local residents and people working nearby began telling stories about strange noises, mysterious shadows and doors that supposedly opened or closed on their own. One especially disturbing claim reported in 2012 was that a security guard assigned to the area was found dead while on duty, although the circumstances of his death do not establish anything supernatural. The legend grew even stronger because people began associating the strange atmosphere of the abandoned properties with the tragic De Monte family. Some locals claimed that the spirits of De Monte and his family still remained around the old residence. Other residents, however, rejected the stories as rumours or mischief, showing that there has never been a single agreed explanation for what supposedly happens there. By the time the **2015 Tamil horror film *Demonte Colony*** became popular, the location had already developed a reputation as one of Chennai's supposedly haunted places. The movie dramatically fictionalized the legend, which made the real location even more famous. In reality, there is no reliable evidence proving that ghosts haunt De Monte Colony. Its frightening reputation appears to have grown from a combination of tragic family history, abandoned properties, local rumours and the eerie appearance of the area.
